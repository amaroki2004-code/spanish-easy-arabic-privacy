# Privacy Policy – Spanish Easy (Arabic)

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains how Spanish Easy (Arabic) ("the App") handles your information.

## 1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personally identifiable information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your lesson progress and settings (such as Kids Mode and difficulty) are stored locally on your device only and are never sent to us.

## 2. Subscriptions & Payments

The App offers optional auto-renewing subscriptions (monthly and yearly), which may include a free trial for new subscribers. Prices are shown in the App and on the App Store in your local currency. All payments are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment or card information.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period (or the free trial). You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your device Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

## 3. Third-Party Services

**Apple In-App Purchase** processes payments. Apple's privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

**RevenueCat** verifies your subscription status using an anonymous, randomly generated ID and Apple's purchase receipt. It does not receive your name, email or contact details. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

We do not use any analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

## 4. Audio

All audio is pre-recorded and stored in the App. The App does not use your microphone and does not record audio.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App includes a Kids Mode for younger learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and no personal data is collected from any user regardless of age.

## 6. Data Security

Because your data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID). Deleting the App removes it.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new date.
